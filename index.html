How is reconstitution volume calculated?

Concentration = peptide mass ÷ water volume. Dose volume = dose ÷ concentration. Syringe units = dose volume × (units per mL). A 1 mL insulin syringe has 100 units, so 1 unit = 0.01 mL.

What is bacteriostatic water?

Bacteriostatic water is sterile water containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol, which inhibits microbial growth. It is used in laboratory research for reconstituting lyophilized (freeze-dried) compounds. This tool performs math — it does not recommend its use for any human application.

What is half-life?

Half-life (t½) is the time required for the concentration of a compound to decrease by 50% via elimination. After ~4–5 half-lives with consistent dosing, plasma concentration approaches steady state.

Research Glossary

Plain-language definitions of terms encountered in peptide research literature. Definitions only — no product recommendations or protocols.

Bioavailability

The fraction of an administered dose that reaches systemic circulation unchanged. Intravenous administration is defined as 100% bioavailable; other routes are compared to it.

Cmax

The maximum observed plasma concentration after administration.

Half-life (t½)

Time required for plasma concentration to decrease by 50% due to elimination.

Lyophilization

Freeze-drying. A preservation method where water is removed from frozen material by sublimation, producing a dry powder that is reconstituted before use.

Reconstitution

The process of dissolving a lyophilized compound in a liquid (typically sterile or bacteriostatic water) to produce a solution.

Steady state

The condition under repeated dosing where the rate of drug input equals the rate of elimination; typically reached after 4–5 half-lives.

Subcutaneous (SC)

Beneath the skin. Subcutaneous injection deposits into the fatty tissue layer below the dermis.

Peptide

A short chain of amino acids linked by peptide bonds, typically fewer than 50 residues.

Bacteriostatic water

Sterile water containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol, which inhibits bacterial growth and allows multi-dose use from a single vial in research contexts.

Volume of distribution (Vd)

A theoretical volume that relates the amount of drug in the body to its plasma concentration.
